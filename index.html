💡 Energy Management Guide & Tips

🔌

1. Sub-Meter CT Ratio & Multiplying Factor (MF)

When connecting machines (Press, Kiln, Compressor, Furnaces) through Current Transformers (CT), the energy meter records secondary current. The Multiplying Factor (MF) is calculated by dividing the primary CT rating by the secondary rating (usually 5A):

CT Specification Calculation Set MF in App
Direct Meter (No CT) Direct reading 1
100 / 5 A CT 100 ÷ 5 20
150 / 5 A CT 150 ÷ 5 30
200 / 5 A CT 200 ÷ 5 40
400 / 5 A CT 400 ÷ 5 80
💡 Crucial Pro-Tip: If your digital Multi-Function Meter (MFM) already has the CT ratio configured internally and displays multiplied actual units on the front LCD screen, enter MF = 1 in the app. Only enter the CT factor (e.g. 20, 40) if your meter displays unmultiplied secondary units.
⚡

2. Main Meter HT MF (Discom Bill)

For High Tension (HT) connections (PGVCL, UGVCL, DGVCL, MGVCL, Torrent Power), the utility meter has an official Multiplying Factor printed on your monthly bill (e.g. 10, 40, 60, 100).

• Formula: Daily Units = (Today's Reading - Yesterday's Reading) × Main MF
• Maximum Demand (kW) is also calculated: Actual MD = Recorded MD × Main MF.
☀️

3. Solar Generation Meter

Enabling solar tracking allows you to monitor solar generation separately from grid consumption:

• Solar MF: Check your solar inverter meter or LT/HT CT ratio (usually 1 for small plants, or matching CT ratio for large solar plants).
• Net Consumption: Grid Import Units - Solar Export Units provides accurate net billing forecasting.
🔄

4. New Meter Reset (Zero Reset Feature)

When an electrical utility meter is replaced or rolled over:

• Enable "Calculate next reading starting from 0" in Factory Settings.
• This prevents negative subtraction from the previous high meter reading.
• The toggle automatically disengages once the initial new entry is saved.
⚡

5. Power Factor (PF) & Maximum Demand (MD) Penalty Rules

🎯 Power Factor (PF) Rebate vs Penalty:
• 🟢 0.950 to 1.000: Optimal PF (Eligible for DISCOM rebate tier on monthly bill).
• 🟠 0.900 to 0.949: Normal Tier (No rebate, no penalty; inspect capacitor banks).
• 🔴 Below 0.900: Low PF Penalty! Check APFC panel and capacitor cells immediately.

⚡ Maximum Demand (MD) Caution & Penalty Thresholds:
• 🟢 Under 95% of Contract Demand: Safe operating range. Ideal power buffer.
• ⚠️ 95% to 99.9% of Contract Demand (High Demand Warning): Warning Alert! Factory is operating very close to sanctioned load. Avoid turning on heavy auxiliary motors simultaneously to prevent entering the penalty tier.
• 🚨 100% & Above (Critical Penalty Risk): Contract Demand exceeded! Heavy penal charges and excess demand tariffs will be billed by DISCOM.
